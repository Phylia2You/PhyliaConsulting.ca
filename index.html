01 / Independent systems project

Phylia CRM & AI workflows

An independent technical project demonstrating connected workflows for inquiries, proposals, customer responses, invoicing, and recurring follow-up.

Python / FlaskPostgreSQLCloudflareAI integration
The problem
Customer requests, correspondence, quotes, and follow-up need a consistent path through a small business.
My contribution
I developed the website and connected CRM workflows using AI-assisted tools, working through configuration, integration, testing, and documentation.
A deliberate decision
AI can prepare a proposal, but a person reviews and approves outbound actions. Generated content never becomes a customer commitment automatically.
Explore the implementation
  • A public intake Worker validates submissions and forwards known fields to an authenticated private API.
  • PostgreSQL stores inquiries, pipeline stages, quote responses, invoice states, and audit events.
  • AI proposal drafts use structured output, validation, editable text, and local fallback templates.
  • Inbound email enters a review workflow. Scheduled jobs handle reminders and suppress duplicate failure notifications.
  • Private receipt storage, migration scripts, focused workflow checks, and deployment notes support ongoing maintenance.
